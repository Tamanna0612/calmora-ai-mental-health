🌟 Overview

Mental health support should be available whenever someone needs it—not only when appointments are available.

Calmora is an AI-powered mental health companion that provides users with a safe, private, and supportive environment to express their thoughts, reflect on their emotions, and develop healthier habits through AI-assisted conversations and wellness tools.

Built with modern web technologies and AI, Calmora combines an intuitive user experience with intelligent backend services to make emotional well-being more accessible.

Mission: Empower people to prioritize their mental wellness through AI-powered, accessible, and compassionate technology.
